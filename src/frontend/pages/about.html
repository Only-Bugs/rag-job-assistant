ResuMate is a fully local AI assistant that generates tailored job application materials from your resume. No cloud. No tracking. No compromises. Every computation happens on your hardware, and your data never leaves your machine.

Philosophy

Why local matters

Three principles that shape every decision we make.

Local-First
Your resume, your skills, your data. Nothing leaves your machine. No cloud servers, no third-party APIs, no tracking. ResuMate runs entirely on your hardware using Ollama for local LLM inference.
Privacy by Design
We don't collect data because there's nothing to collect. No accounts stored on servers, no analytics, no telemetry. Your application materials are yours alone — always.
Open & Transparent
Built with open-source tools: LangChain for orchestration, ChromaDB for vector search, and Ollama for local LLM inference. No black boxes, no hidden layers, no proprietary lock-in.
